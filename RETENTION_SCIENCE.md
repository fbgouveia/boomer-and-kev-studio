# Audiovisual Retention Science & Neuromarketing
**Purpose:** Core directives for audience retention in short-form videos (TikTok, Shorts, Reels).

## 1. The Amygdala Hijack (First 3 Seconds)
The amygdala is the brain's threat/reward processor. It bypasses logic and acts instantly.
*   **The Hook:** Start mid-sentence, mid-action, or with an aggressive visual/auditory spike. No "hello" or "welcome back".
*   **Visual Pattern Interrupt:** Use high-contrast movement, extreme angles, or unexpected elements instantly to force the brain to stop scrolling.

## 2. Anticipation Loops
The human brain is wired to seek cognitive closure.
*   Present a high-stakes problem, bold claim, or tension in Scene 1.
*   **Delay the resolution** until the final scene.
*   If the brain knows the answer immediately, it swipes away. If there's an "open loop," it stays until the end.

## 3. Combating Neural Habituation
The brain gets bored (habituates) to repetitive stimuli within 3-4 seconds.
*   **Pattern Interrupts:** Change camera angles (Wide to Extreme Close Up), shift pacing, or change audio profiles constantly.
*   **Mixed Emotions:** Shift rapidly from tension to comedy, or confusion to realization. A flat emotional tone kills retention.

## 4. The Looping Payoff
*   **Abrupt Ending:** End the video the exact millisecond the punchline or value is delivered.
*   Do not say "thanks for watching" or "subscribe". 
*   A sudden cut forces the video to loop before the viewer realizes it ended, artificially boosting retention metrics.
