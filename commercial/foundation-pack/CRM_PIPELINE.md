# COMMERCIAL PIPELINE

## Stages

1. **Lead** — identified, no contact yet
2. **Qualified** — brand-fit score completed
3. **Discovery** — business problem and rights discussed
4. **Brief received** — minimum brief complete
5. **Proposal sent** — scope, rights and fee dated
6. **Negotiation** — open issues recorded
7. **Contracted** — signature and deposit received
8. **Production** — approval owner and dates active
9. **Delivered/published**
10. **Reporting** — 7/30-day report due
11. **Renewal**
12. **Closed/lost** — reason recorded
