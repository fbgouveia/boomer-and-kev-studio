# CLIENT OFFER MENU — v0

All pricing is quoted after briefing. Publication, paid media, exclusivity and
usage rights are not automatically included in production fees.

## Integration Test

Best for: a first collaboration with low operational risk.

Includes:

- one native brand integration within an agreed Boomer & Kev episode;
- one factual/claims review;
- publication on one agreed organic channel;
- basic performance report at 7 and 30 days.

Excludes by default:

- paid amplification;
- category exclusivity;
- brand-channel usage;
- raw or project files.

## Branded Sketch

Best for: a product that can trigger a visible comic conflict.

Includes:

- creative concept and script;
- one vertical master;
- two alternative opening hooks;
- product demonstration or proof beat;
- CTA and disclosure treatment;
- two consolidated revision rounds.

## Campaign System

Best for: brands that need a learning system rather than one asset.

Includes:

- campaign concept and hypothesis map;
- three to five controlled creative variants;
- agreed 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and/or 16:9 outputs;
- hook, offer or CTA differentiation log;
- campaign handover and performance-report template.

## Season Partner

Best for: a recurring partner with strong audience and category fit.

Includes:

- fixed episode inventory;
- environmental brand presence where agreed;
- monthly creative and reporting cadence;
- time-limited category exclusivity;
- mutual brand-safety exit clause.

## Character/IP Collaboration

Best for: co-created merchandise, licensed products or long-running campaigns.

Requires:

- separate creative and rights approval;
- defined territory, term, channels and category;
- minimum guarantee and/or royalty structure;
- quality-control rights;
- no permanent transfer of Boomer & Kev IP.
