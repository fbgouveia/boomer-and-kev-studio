# BOOMER & KEV

## Campaigns built to entertain.

Boomer & Kev is an Australian entertainment property built around an explosive
kangaroo, a dry-witted koala and one repeatable truth: the product should not
interrupt the episode — it should provoke it.

Boomer creates desire, urgency and chaos. Kev voices the objection the audience
is already thinking. The product enters through action, demonstration and
payoff, so branded content still earns attention as entertainment.

## What we make

- Native sponsor integrations inside Boomer & Kev episodes
- Dedicated branded comedy sketches
- Vertical performance creative with controlled hook and CTA variants
- Multi-format campaign systems for organic and paid channels
- Character and IP collaborations

## Why the format works

- A recognisable comic duo, not a replaceable presenter
- Conflict makes the product part of the story
- Short-form pacing designed for retention and replay
- Multiple hooks and formats from one approved campaign idea
- Human review for brand facts, claims, character and disclosure
