# THREE CONCEPT CAMPAIGNS

All brands and products below are fictional demonstrations. They are not client
work and must retain a visible concept disclosure.

## Concept 01 — RooFuel Cold Brew

Category: coffee / non-alcoholic beverage  
Objective: consideration  
Audience: Australian commuters and shift workers  
Working line: **Energy for people who have already had enough.**

### Product truth used for the concept

Fictional placeholder: ready-to-drink cold brew with a simple caffeine amount
printed clearly on pack. Any real execution must replace this with substantiated
brand facts.

### Comic engine

Boomer decides ordinary coffee is too weak and builds an absurd “outback
performance test”. Kev points out that the actual benefit is convenience:
the drink is cold, ready and does not require Boomer’s dangerous machine.

### Story beats

1. Hook: Boomer slams an oversized alarm clock off the desk.
2. Chaos: he connects boxing equipment to a homemade coffee rig.
3. Objection: Kev refuses the contraption and opens the canned cold brew.
4. Proof: clear product close-up and real preparation/use moment.
5. Payoff: Boomer’s machine destroys the alarm clock; Kev is already leaving.
6. CTA: try the straightforward option.

### Hook variants

- A: “Coffee should fight back.”
- B: “Boomer has solved mornings. Unfortunately.”
- C: cold open on the failed machine, then reveal the cause.

### Controlled test

Variable: verbal hook versus visual consequence.  
Hold constant: product proof, payoff, CTA and duration.

### Outputs

- 20–25 second 9:16 master;
- three openings;
- 6-second proof/CTA cutdown;
- 1:1 thumbnail/key visual.

## Concept 02 — Bushproof Multi-Tool

Category: outdoor / tools  
Objective: awareness and consideration  
Audience: campers, tradies and practical-gift buyers  
Working line: **One tool. Fewer Boomer solutions.**

### Product truth used for the concept

Fictional placeholder: compact multi-tool with a defined set of functions.
Every shown function must exist on the real product.

### Comic engine

Boomer brings an entire toolbox to repair one loose studio fixture. Kev solves
the problem with one multi-tool before Boomer finishes unloading.

### Story beats

1. Hook: tools crash onto the desk one after another.
2. Tension: one loose screw has stopped the recording.
3. Chaos: Boomer proposes drilling through the wall.
4. Proof: Kev selects and uses the correct tool visibly.
5. Payoff: recording resumes while Boomer is trapped under his toolbox.
6. CTA: pack capability, not chaos.

### Hook variants

- A: “This repair requires 47 tools.”
- B: visual cascade of tools before either character speaks.
- C: Kev silently fixes the problem while Boomer explains the plan.

### Controlled test

Variable: dialogue-first versus action-first opening.  
Hold constant: demonstrated function, product close-up and CTA.

### Outputs

- 25–30 second 9:16 master;
- two hooks;
- 15-second paid cutdown;
- 16:9 version for brand and YouTube use.

## Concept 03 — Snoozr Focus App

Category: consumer technology / app  
Objective: trial  
Audience: students and knowledge workers who procrastinate  
Working line: **Focus without pretending you became a different person.**

### Product truth used for the concept

Fictional placeholder: a focus timer that blocks selected distractions for a
user-defined session. A real execution must use verified UI and functionality.

### Comic engine

Boomer designs a militarised productivity system. Kev activates one short focus
session, completes the task and schedules a nap.

### Story beats

1. Hook: “Kev has done nothing for three hours.”
2. Reversal: Kev reveals the finished task.
3. Chaos: Boomer unveils a wall-sized productivity command centre.
4. Proof: real screen capture of starting a focus session.
5. Payoff: the app blocks Boomer’s motivational notifications.
6. CTA: start one focused session.

### Hook variants

- A: accusation-first dialogue;
- B: completed-task reveal before context;
- C: notification storm interrupted by the app.

### Controlled test

Variable: character conflict versus UI-first proof.  
Hold constant: verified feature, trial CTA and disclosure.

### Outputs

- 20-second 9:16 master;
- three hooks;
- UI-proof cutdown;
- static 4:5 CTA asset.

## Selection rule

The first case to produce should be the one that can show the clearest honest
proof with the fewest third-party dependencies. Current recommendation:
**Bushproof Multi-Tool**, because the product benefit is physical, immediate and
understandable without claims-heavy copy.
