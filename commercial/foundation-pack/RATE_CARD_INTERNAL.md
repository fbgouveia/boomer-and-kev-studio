## Quote structure

Every quote separates:

1. development and production;
2. publication/distribution;
3. brand usage licence;
4. paid amplification or whitelisting;
5. category exclusivity;
6. third-party costs.

## Internal adjustment factors

### Usage

- Brand organic channels, 3 months: add 20–35% of production
- Brand organic channels, 6 months: add 35–50%
- Paid media, 3 months: add 50–100%
- Paid media renewal: reprice from the original licence, not production
- Perpetual/all-media request: do not accept as standard; escalate

### Exclusivity

- Narrow category, 30 days: add 15–25%
- Narrow category, 90 days: add 30–50%
- Broad category or longer term: custom opportunity-cost calculation

### Production

- Additional hook from approved concept: fixed incremental fee
- New concept or material script direction: new scope
- Rush delivery: add 25–50%
- Extra revision round: quoted before work resumes
- Raw/project files: separate fee and licence

## Payment position

- 50% on signature
- 50% before final delivery or publication
- Media and third-party costs prepaid
- Cancellation after concept approval triggers a kill fee

## Non-negotiables

- no guaranteed views, virality, sales or ROAS;
- no invented or unsubstantiated claim;
- no undisclosed commercial relationship;
- no permanent transfer of character IP;
- no unpaid speculative full concept execution.
