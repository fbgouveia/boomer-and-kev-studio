## External-distribution blockers

Do not send the pack externally until these are confirmed:

1. commercial contact email;
2. legal entity and invoicing details;
3. founder title and public role;
4. canonical public naming: Boomer & Kev versus Down Under Discourse;
5. approved price position;
6. verified channel and audience metrics, when available;
7. legal review of the final client agreement.

The deck intentionally says **pre-launch v0**, includes no fabricated audience
statistics and marks concept artwork as internal demonstration.
