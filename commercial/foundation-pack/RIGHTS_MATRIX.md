# COMMERCIAL RIGHTS MATRIX

This is a scoping tool, not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed
agreement.

| Right | Included by default? | Must specify |
|---|---|---|
| Production of agreed assets | Yes | Deliverables, formats, revisions |
| Publication on Boomer & Kev channels | No | Account, platform, date, duration |
| Brand organic usage | No | Channels, territory, term |
| Paid media usage | No | Platform, spend context, territory, term |
| Spark Ads / Partnership Ads / whitelisting | No | Account access, authorisation term |
| Category exclusivity | No | Competitor set, territory, term |
| Edit/cutdown rights | No | Permitted changes and approval |
| Raw/project files | No | Files, fee, technical support |
| Character/name/voice licence | No | Exact use, term, territory, category |
| Merchandise rights | No | Products, approvals, minimums, royalties |
| Portfolio/case-study use | Requested | Timing, confidential data, brand approval |
| Perpetual/all-media ownership | No | Escalate; not a standard offer |

## Approval split

**Brand controls**

- factual product information;
- substantiated claims;
- offer, price and availability;
- trademarks and supplied assets;
- legal disclaimers.

**Boomer & Kev controls**

- character behaviour and voice;
- comedy, pacing and editorial treatment;
- final creative quality;
- property safety;
- disclosure implementation.

## Contract checklist

- scope and delivery dates;
- fees, deposit and pass-through costs;
- revision rounds;
- approval windows;
- cancellation and kill fee;
- licence start and end dates;
- territory and platforms;
- paid versus organic use;
- exclusivity;
- claims warranties and indemnities;
- music and third-party assets;
- AI-assisted production disclosure where required;
- performance metrics without guarantees;
- portfolio rights;
- crisis, takedown and termination;
- ownership of Boomer & Kev IP.
